What counts as e-waste

If it has a plug, a battery or a circuit, we'll take it

E-waste is collected separately from general household waste, because it needs different handling — some components shouldn't go anywhere near a landfill.

  • Old phones, tablets, laptops and chargers.
  • Batteries, cables and small circuit boards.
  • Small appliances — irons, mixers, old routers.
  • TVs and monitors (let us know the size when booking).

Why it's handled separately

Electronics don't belong in a general bin

Keeps it out of landfill

Circuit boards and batteries can leach into soil and water if dumped with ordinary trash.

Recovers what's reusable

Metals and parts inside old devices can be recovered instead of mined again from scratch.

One less thing to figure out

No need to research the nearest e-waste drop-off point — we come to you instead.
