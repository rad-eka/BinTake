What's included

A pickup that fits your week, not the other way around

Doorstep Waste Collection covers general household waste — kitchen scraps, packaging, everyday refuse — collected directly from outside your gate or doorstep, at a time you choose.

  • General household waste, collected from your gate — no walk to a communal bin.
  • Flexible scheduling — one-off, daily, or a fixed weekly rhythm.
  • A confirmed arrival window, so you're not left guessing.
  • Waste sorted and routed responsibly after collection.

Pricing

The exact price depends on the volume of waste and your locality. Weekly plans work out cheaper per pickup.
